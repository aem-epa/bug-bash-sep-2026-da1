Why loose-leaf?

Tea bags are usually filled with the leftover dust and fannings of a harvest. Whole leaves give you the real thing — more aroma, more flavor, and cups you can steep again.

Whole leaves, never dust

We steep only full and broken-leaf grades, so every cup tastes rounder and re-steeps two or three times.

Roasted & packed weekly

Small batches mean nothing sits in a warehouse. Most blends reach you within three weeks of packing.

Gardens we know by name

We buy direct from twelve family gardens and pay above the fair-trade floor for every kilo.
