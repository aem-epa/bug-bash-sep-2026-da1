Sourcing that starts with people

We skip the auction houses and buy straight from twelve family gardens across China, Japan, India and Sri Lanka. Because we know each grower, we can pay above the fair-trade floor, commit to multi-year orders, and invest in the next harvest before it's picked.

That stability lets our partners farm for the long term — protecting old tea bushes, shade trees and the watersheds around them.

From garden to your cup

Four commitments that shape every order.

Partner directly

We work hand-in-hand with growers, visiting gardens each season rather than buying blind at auction.

Pay above fair-trade

Every kilo is bought at a premium over the fair-trade floor, with prices agreed openly and in advance.

Ship by sea

Tea travels by ocean freight, not air, cutting the carbon footprint of each tin by up to 95%.

Pack plastic-free

Compostable liners, recyclable tins and paper tape — designed to leave nothing behind.

Pledges we hold ourselves to

Plastic-free packaging

Every component of our packaging is compostable or widely recyclable.

Carbon-neutral shipping

We measure and offset the footprint of every delivery through verified projects.

Regenerative farming

1% of revenue funds soil health, shade trees and biodiversity at partner gardens.

Fair, living wages

We verify that garden workers are paid a living wage, reviewed independently each year.
